🔴 Critical Actions
⚠
Commission Structure is Your #1 Profit Killer
Commission/Brokerage at ₹8.2L YTD is the single largest controllable expense (25.5% of net sales). Jan alone was ₹4.72L — largely tied to HappyFest deal. Renegotiate to % of gross margin, not gross revenue. Even a 15% reduction saves ₹1.2L/month.
CRITICAL
⚡
Single-Channel B2B Dependency Risk
CDC Marketplace alone = 39% of YTD gross revenue. If CDC reduces orders or renegotiates terms, you lose ₹22L+ revenue. Start diversifying — target 2 new B2B retail partners per quarter and cap any single partner at 25% of revenue.
CRITICAL
🟡 High Priority
📸
Photoshoot Expenses Need a Content Calendar
₹97,800 in Jan vs ₹40,000 in Feb — 145% variance. Batch shoots quarterly instead of monthly. A planned 3-month content calendar could reduce annual photoshoot cost by 30–40% (saving ₹4–6L/year).
HIGH PRIORITY
🎯
Size M is Consistently Your Hero SKU — Lean In
Size M drives ₹1.24L (31%) of Jan Shopify revenue in 37 units. Yet XXL (17 units) is the second-highest volume. Ensure M + XXXL are always in stock. Running out of M = direct revenue loss. Increase safety stock by 40%.
HIGH PRIORITY
🔄
Repeat Customer Revenue is Declining
Jan: Repeat customers contributed ₹99.9K (25.3%) of B2C. Feb: ₹72.7K (17.1%) — a 27% drop. Activate a loyalty program or WhatsApp re-engagement campaign. Even converting 20% of new buyers into repeat buyers adds ₹40K+/month.
HIGH PRIORITY
💳
COD Rate Dropping Rapidly — Capitalise on It
Jan COD = 39.5% of B2C revenue. Feb = 13.5%. This is excellent (COD has higher return risk). Offer an additional 3–5% prepaid discount via PREPAID5 or new codes to further reduce COD to sub-10%. Saves RTO costs and improves cash flow.
HIGH PRIORITY
🟢 Growth Opportunities
🗺
Karnataka is Your Fastest Growing B2C State
KA jumped from ₹2,199 (Jan) to ₹37,083 (Feb) — a 16× surge. This is likely organic word-of-mouth. Allocate targeted Meta/Instagram ads to Bangalore + Mysore. The ₹37K base can be scaled to ₹1.5L+/month within 2 months with ₹15K ad spend.
OPPORTUNITY
🏟
Event Channel is Your Highest Margin Play
Dubai Arena, Team Innovation, and Innovation Nightlife all had 99–100% gross margins on services (design charges, event management). B2B event revenue with near-zero COGS is your ideal channel. Build a dedicated events sales pipeline — target 3–4 events/month at ₹5–15L each.
OPPORTUNITY
📱
Facebook is 20% of Revenue at Low Acquisition Cost
Facebook drove ₹78.4K (Jan) and ₹96.2K (Feb) in Shopify revenue — 23 orders and 19 orders respectively. At ₹1.4L total ad spend, the ROI is approximately 5.6× ROAS. Increase Facebook budget by 30% (₹42K/mo more) to capture this efficiently scaling channel.
OPPORTUNITY
⭐
High-Margin Products Need Priority Inventory Allocation
7 of top 10 Jan products have 85%+ margins (TOO MUCH Sweatshirt, 777 Jersey, Head In The Clouds = 100%). These are likely older inventory with COGS already written off. Allocate photoshoot and ad budget specifically to promote these — selling ₹1 of 100% margin product is better than ₹1.5 of 70% margin product.

🌏
International B2B Channel (Dubai) Needs Expansion
Dubai Arena Operations LLC is a single ₹4.31L invoice from February with massive potential. Luxury streetwear in UAE, Singapore, and the UK is growing rapidly. One international deal per month at ₹5–10L adds ₹60–1.2Cr to annual revenue with minimal COGS.

Activate FD Interest as Working Capital Lever
FD interest income of ₹43,785 YTD is healthy. Consider growing FD corpus — if EBITDA allows, park ₹30–40L in high-yield FDs (6.5–7%) to generate ₹2–2.5L/year in passive income that partially offsets fixed costs like rent (₹1.8L/year).
